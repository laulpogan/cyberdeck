# Doctrine — sound, motion, and the spatial cut

Three doctrines that came out of the wide pass and have not been contradicted
since. They are recorded here because they constrain components that do not exist
yet, and a constraint nobody wrote down is a constraint nobody will honour.

Source page: [`pages/the-idea-bank.html`](pages/the-idea-bank.html). Restated as
Part 0 of [`source-archive.md`](source-archive.md), where it overrides every
property-specific note in that file.

## Motion

The library's rule — motion is a measurement or it does not happen — is the
enforced half. These are the parts that sit above it as taste rather than as gate.

- Motion means state change and nothing else. Idle animation is permitted in
  exactly two ceremonies: boot, and landing.
- Springs on instruments run 150–250 ms. Scrubbing a timeline carries flywheel
  inertia. Interrupts are hard cuts — an interruption that animates politely is
  lying about what just happened.
- Choreography is spent once per session. One boot reveal, then stillness. A
  reveal never gates content.
- Glitch grammar is semantic. It is reserved for canonical-state corruption and
  for marking an agent's speculation. Used decoratively it stops meaning either.
- Phosphor decay belongs to anything historical. Live data never trails.

## Sound

Radios and instruments, not notification chimes. Silence is the nominal state.

- Decision proximity uses a motion-tracker cadence: the ping interval is the
  urgency. One instrument, one sound.
- Ambient hum sits under live fleets. Full silence means everything landed —
  quiet is the reward, not the failure mode.
- Landing is a single tape-stop clunk. Acceptance is a bolt turn. Refusal is a dry
  relay click. All mechanical, all under 200 ms.
- The critical class gets the only sustained alarm, and it is unmutable by design.
- All ambience ducks when a decision lands.

## The spatial cut

Target is standalone WebXR at 72–90 Hz and roughly 20 pixels per degree. Text is
the scarce resource, and every constraint below follows from that.

**Geometry**

- The field curves as a 210° wall at 2.5 m — opsroom geometry — with cells no
  smaller than 2° of visual angle.
- The timeline runs as a physical tape table at waist height: grab, stretch, pinch
  a seal to open its receipt.
- The organism explodes in hand space. Grab a fleet, open it like an assembly.
- The decision queue lives on the wrist, glance-triggered, read aloud, committed
  with a physical twist.
- Panes hand off by flick: wrist → desk → room wall.

**Hard constraints**

- Text floor is about 1.5° cap height. A mono face holds up at that size; a display
  serif does not. Titles become signage, not paragraphs.
- Phosphor-on-black is the right palette for OLED and the wrong one for an LCD
  headset. Lift blacks toward #0A0A08 and spend bloom sparingly.
- Three canvas instruments is the frame budget. The comb renders as an instanced
  mesh, never as DOM.
- No locomotion. Everything comes to the operator; scope descent happens seated.
- Sessions run about 25 minutes. Design the shift ritual at both ends.

**Honesty in three dimensions**

- Depth is a claim. Only measured relationships earn z-separation; unmeasured stays
  flat and prints UNMEASURED.
- Presence is a claim. An agent gets a face only where its identity is
  cryptographically verified. Otherwise it is featureless glass.
- A room lies more easily than a page, so every skin is stamped with the name of
  the lens it is showing.
