# Does the switch move the page?

Measured against the running app at 1280px, all 51 components: the
`[data-specimen-view]` box on both sides of the rack's own evidence switch,
`app/verify/pair-heights.mjs`. The rule is **asymmetric**:

> A refusal may be **shorter** than the measurement — an unanswered list has one line in it,
> and padding it out would be inventing questions. It may not be **taller** by more than
> 26px, which is one refusal sentence: the only thing a refusal is obliged to add.

Growing is ink the measurement never claimed, and it moves everything below the card because
somebody changed an epistemic state. Shrinking is a quantity.

**0 of 51 specimens grow past the allowance** — none.

## Shrinking is allowed, and here is what it costs

**3 specimens are shorter refused than measured by more than the ±26px band.** Each is a truthful quantity —
the container survives, there is simply less in it — but the page below still moves up, which
is the trade the rule chooses deliberately: reserving the measured height would put blank space
where an absence is being declared, and a reserved box reads as a quiet measurement.

| component | measured | refused | shrink | why it is legitimate |

| `muthur` | 414px | 201px | -213px | one unasked prompt where four answered queries were; the console frame is kept |
| `individuation` | 314px | 157px | -157px | one tank observed, no siblings — the row it replaces is one row |
| `tape` | 275px | 146px | -129px | nothing is queued, so the tape is one blank slot |

The remaining 48 specimens hold within ±26px. One of them changed while finding #9 was being
closed: `stripChart` went from 0px to **-14px** refused, because the lane that used to carry
the words `ONE SAMPLE` now carries the retained series, and the sentence about having one
sample is no longer a sentence this drawing gets to say. A refusal that stops applying should
get smaller — that is the quantity half of the rule working, not drifting.

`scaleCrush` reads -2px instead of the 0px it held when the fixed-size refusal was written.
Two pixels at one width is rounding in the specimen fit, not a growth, and it stays inside the
band the rule sets.
